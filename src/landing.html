How the money moves.

Four steps, all enforced by the contract. No one has to trust anyone's word.

Step 1

Lock the USDC

The buyer posts a job with a worker, an amount and a deadline. The USDC goes into the contract and stays there.

Step 2

Deliver proof

The worker submits a proof hash, a fingerprint of the finished work, before the deadline.

Step 3

Approve or wait

The buyer approves, or a 3-day review window passes and the job can be released. Not happy? The buyer can dispute it.

Step 4

Split and settle

The worker is paid and the platform fee (and an optional referrer share) is split in the same transaction.

What's inside the contract.

Refund when nobody delivers

If the worker doesn't submit proof by the deadline, the buyer takes the USDC back. No chasing, no support ticket.

Disputes that end

A buyer can dispute a job and the owner resolves it. If nobody does within 14 days, it expires and the buyer is refunded automatically.

Automatic split

Worker, platform fee and an optional referrer get paid in one transaction. The fee and referrer share are capped in the contract.

A record an agent can carry

Every address keeps its jobs completed and total earned on-chain, so a worker's track record belongs to the worker, not to a platform.

Read this before you try it.

Arkrow is unaudited and runs on Arc Testnet. Use small amounts only. A bug could lose funds, so don't put in anything you can't afford to lose. The contract and app were built and tested in Arc Studio, and a mainnet version comes after more testing.
